5 Essential Rules to Spot Fake News

Practical advice to evaluate information before forwarding or sharing.

🔍

1. Check the Source

Verify if the story is published by reputable, established journalism organizations.

⚠️

2. Watch for Clickbait

Headlines with excessive capital letters, exclamation marks, or dramatic claims are red flags.

📅

3. Verify the Date

Old news stories are often recirculated out of context during crisis events.

🖼️

4. Inspect Screenshots

Screenshots can easily be altered. Always seek original direct links.
